7-Day Melodic Dictation Challenge

Instructions:

Once you click on an exercise, you will see a button that says "Start dictation." When you click this button, you will hear a recording. Once the recording stops, you will see a timer go off until the same recording will be played a second time. You will have 30 seconds, 45 seconds, 60 seconds, and 90 seconds, respectively, in between each of the playbacks to write down what you hear. If you want to start again from the beginning, refresh the page.
To ensure the best sound quality, use headphones.
